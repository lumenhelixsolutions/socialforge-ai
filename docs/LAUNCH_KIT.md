## Demo script

1. Open the app.
2. Create a social post task.
3. Generate draft content.
4. Edit the platform preview.
5. Review the task.
6. Approve it.
7. Schedule it locally.
8. Inspect the history.
9. Create a raw creative task.
10. Show that raw output cannot be scheduled directly.
11. Promote raw output into a safe reviewed task.
